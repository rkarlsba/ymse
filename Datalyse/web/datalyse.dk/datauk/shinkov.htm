Shinko balances

New driver February 2003

Menu in Datalyse:

Graphical measurement

The balance has two setting: fast weighing and stable weighing.

At fast weighing the display reading is transmitted to the pc immediately. At stable weighing the reading is not transmitted until the balance is stable.

Minimum times: 2 seconds / 10 seconds.

Note that the cable is crossed:

2 to 3 and 3 to 2 (data)
5 to 20 and 20 to 5 (control), if the connector is 25 pin.

You can also use the cable for Digital Meter or 2001 timer -counter.

Baud rate 1200, no parity, 2 stop, 8 data bits.

If the communication does not work, check the settings using the buttons on the front panel.

No version command.
