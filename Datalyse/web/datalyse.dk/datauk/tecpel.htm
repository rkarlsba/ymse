Graphical measurement

The TECPEL 331 Sound Level Meter is capable of measuring sound in the interval from 30 dB to 130 dB. Precision: 1.5 dB

It has a serial interface and it has an analog dc output. You can use the dc output in connection with a fast datalogger to measure reverberation times.

Baud rate 9600, no parity, 1 stop, 8 data bits. Cable is included.
