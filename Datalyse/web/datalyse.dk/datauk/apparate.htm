Having chosen a device from the main menu of Datalyse, the menu of the device will be merged into the main menu. You now have several topics to choose between, including:

  • Multimeter: pH or voltage measurements.
  • Energymeter: Setup the datalog, read the datalog, summary measurement.
  • Function generator: Frequency measurement, control panel.
  • Ratemeter: Half-life, half-thickness, read the datalog.
  • Counter: Elastic and inelastic collision, half-life, half-thickness etc.

to mention but a few of the possibilities.
