    DMI4 multimeter, version 1.20

    Menu in Datalyse

    Graphical measurement of a value.
    Multitable of from 1 to 4 values.

    The demonstration multimeter can measure current, voltage, resistance, frequency, temperature, pH and pressure.
    There are four galvanically separated parts in the DMI4. This feature makes it possible to leave measurement probes connected while measuring something else. And it is possible to measure temperature, pH and pressure and Voltage/current/resistance/frequency at the same time when the device is connected to a computer.

    The 4 sections are as follows:

    1. Temperature
    2. pH
    3. Pressure
    4. Volt, current, resistance and frequency

    With Graphical measurement turn the central knob to the wanted function.

    With measurements in Multitable turn the central knob to Volt, current, resistance or frequency if yo want to measure one of these quantities.

    Baud rate 9600, no parity, 1 stop, 8 data bits. Command "V" for version.

    DT4 temperature meter med datalog, version 1.10

    Menu in Datalyse:

    Graphical measurement of one, chosen parameter.

    EM1 energy meter with datalog

    Menu in Datalyse

    Graphical measurement.
    Multitable in which all parameters are measured and viewed in a table.
    Summary. Measurements are not saved.
    Setup log. Setup of all parameters.
    Reading the log.

    The log is written to the table, graphs can be made later on.

    Baud rate 1200 (9600), no parity, 1 stop, 8 data bits. Command "V" for version.

    The 9600 in brackets means that Datalyse sets this baud rate at startup. This reduces the log reading time to an acceptable 30 seconds.

    Notice that there is no menu (command) for resetting the log; it is reset when beginning a new measurement after having setup the log.

    Setup log

    Start condition:

    1: manual
    2: clock
    3: power level

    The log is started manually by simultaneously pressing FUNCTION and RESET/SELECT buttons on the Energy Meter. If you choose clock, you do not have to specify a power level. If you choose power level, there is no need to specify a starting time.

    Log layout:

    1: automatic compression
    2: preset time

    The log may hold 250 measurements.

    Automatic compression means that every second measurement will be deleted when the log is full, making room for more measurements. If you choose preset time, the time between each measurement is calculated from the total logging time. Logging ends when the datalog is full.

    During the log setup, the log will be readied for measuring. The datalog will not be deleted until the log is activated.

    FD4E function generator, version 1.21

    Menu in Datalyse:

    Graphical measurement of frequencies.
    FD4E setup, for control of all of FD4E's functions.

    Baud rate 1200 (9600), no parity, 1 stop, 8 data bits. Command "V" for version.

    The 9600 in brackets means that Datalyse sets this baud rate at startup. Otherwise, using the control panel with FD4E would be too slow.

    control:

    Setup:

    You can control the overtones of a standing cord. Enter the frequency and press »Overtones«. The device will be set to 1st overtone, 2nd overtone...9th overtone and again the base tone.

    Be aware that setting the FD4E to a certain frequency is much easier (faster) with the setup panel than the buttons on front of the device.

    Guess a note:

    Random provides you with a tone. Try guessing the frequency; press Answer to see the proper answer.

    Sweep:

    FD4E does not have a hardware sweep but you can make a sweep with Datalyse. The parameters are set here and the sweep is started from the main menu under »Start sweep«. Datalyse sends a frequency command to FD4E every time a time step has passed (minimum: 0,05s, maximum: 5,0 s). Before starting you have to set FD4E to Amplifier and an appropriate volume. If the pc has a soundcard the checkbox »Measure sound« will be active. This means that you can measure a sound signal as a function of frequency. E.g. you can show resonance in a cardboard tube.

    The pc's microphone has an interval of 16 bits [-32767; 32768]. While measuring Datalyse finds the minimum and the maximum of the microphone signal and calculates the amplitude in %.

    MI-multiinterface

    Menu in Datalyse:

    Graphical measurement of one parameter.
    Multitable, a number of parameters are measured.
    Process scheme of 2 independent circuits.

    MI-multiinterface is useful for process scheming, but only with a PC.

    Inputs:

    One 0- 200 mV.
    One 0-2000 mV.
    Three digital inputs (0-5V).
    Two temperature inputs (-150 °C - 1400 °C).

    Outputs:

    Two digital outputs (0-5V) via opto-couplers.
    Two relay switches (0-250V) and max 5A

    Baud rate 1200, even parity, 1 stop, 7 data bits. Command "V" for version.

    Process scheme:

    Circuit 1 outputs will be high until the temperature is above 30°C. Then the outputs will remain low until the temperature is below 22°C. Like a thermostat. In this picture, the temperature has been above 30 °C.

    As you see, in circuit 2, the first voltage is greater than the second. This has the opposite effect. The output will become high when the input is greater than the start value. The output will be low when the input goes below the stop value, and it will not be high again until the input is once again greater than the start value. Just like a refrigerator.

    At least one input and one output must be chosen (in the same circuit).

    Also notice the other information about high/min: 0.76 67%

    and the total processing time.
