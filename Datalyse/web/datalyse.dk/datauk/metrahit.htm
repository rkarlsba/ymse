MetraHIT multimeter

Menu in Datalyse:

Graphical measurement
Multitable, measuring on any number from 1 to 6 of connected MetraHITs

MetraHIT provides you with a high level of safety: Continuous, doubled or reinforced insulation, over voltage category III or IV and the multimeter is equipped with an automatic socket blocking device.

Along with an adapter with infrared communication you can use it for data collection. The adapter is connected to the pc with a regular serial cable. When collecting data, Datalyse will automatically turn MetraHIT on! Does it get any easier? It also read device type and battery voltage.

MetraHIT can measure voltage, current, resistance, frequency, temperature and capacity. The display has 5 digits and the meter has auto range.

When selecting Multitable you can measure on any number from 1 to 6 of connected MeltraHITs.

If several multimeters, interfaces or memory adapters are connected to the pc, each device requires its own address. Address number 1 should be assigned to the first device, 2 to the second device, etc.

Baud rate 9600, even parity, 1 stop, 8 data bits.
